The problem

Food businesses are simulating a cold chain.

Many smaller food ecommerce businesses currently rely on normal parcel couriers combined with insulated boxes, liners, ice packs and gel packs.

It works — but it adds cost, packaging, weight and operational complexity, while failed or delayed deliveries can be particularly painful when the order contains perishable food.

We think there may be a better way: use active refrigerated transport and make the technology layer accessible to smaller ecommerce businesses.

A different model

Not just cheaper delivery.

The useful comparison isn't simply courier price. It's the total cost of getting an order to the customer successfully.

The concept

Connect demand with refrigerated capacity.

No Time To Sleep wouldn't become another refrigerated courier. The idea is to build the technology and network layer between merchants and existing refrigerated operators.

01

Food business

A merchant receives chilled or frozen ecommerce orders through Shopify or another platform.

02

Technology layer

Order data, postcode, weight, temperature requirement and delivery date are passed into the platform.

03

Refrigerated carrier

Suitable deliveries are allocated to available refrigerated operators and multi-drop routes.

→ Long-term platform

Shopify → platform → refrigerated carrier → customer

Over time the platform could manage carrier selection, labels, manifests, tracking, customer notifications, proof of delivery, temperature information, billing and claims.
