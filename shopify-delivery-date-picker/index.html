FAQ

Shopify delivery date picker questions.

Where does the TruBytes date picker appear?

The current storefront date-selection block is designed for the Shopify cart page before checkout. Cart-drawer and product-page date selection are not currently supported.

Can different products have different preparation times?

Yes. Product-specific lead-time rules can apply to selected products or reusable product groups, so the basket can change the earliest valid fulfilment date.

Can I limit how many orders are accepted for one day?

Yes. TruBytes supports daily order limits, product quantity limits and time-slot capacity so merchants can avoid accepting more work than they can fulfil.

Does TruBytes support local delivery and pickup?

Yes. Shipping, local delivery and pickup can each have their own availability. Local delivery can use postcode or radius rules, while pickup can use configured collection locations and slots.

Can customers choose a date inside Shopify checkout?

The public app currently uses a cart-page block. Checkout placement is not currently offered in the public version.
