FAQ

Bakery & cake shops delivery date questions.

Can different cakes have different preparation times?

Yes. TruBytes can apply product-specific lead times, so the basket changes the earliest available delivery or pickup date.

Can I limit how many cakes can be ordered for one day?

Yes. Daily product quantity limits can cap individual products, while order and time-slot limits control the wider production day.

Can some cakes be pickup-only?

Yes. Method-restriction rules can limit selected products to shipping, local delivery, pickup, or a combination of those methods.

Does TruBytes work with multiple pickup locations?

Yes. Pickup locations can have their own available days, lead-time adjustments, blackout dates and time slots.

Where does the TruBytes storefront picker appear?

The current public app uses a Shopify cart-page app block before checkout. Cart-drawer, product-page and checkout date selection are not currently supported.
