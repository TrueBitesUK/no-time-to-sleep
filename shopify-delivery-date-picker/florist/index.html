Florists

The problem is not taking orders. It is taking too many for the same day.

A florist can go from a normal week to a huge spike in orders around Valentine’s Day or Mother’s Day. The calendar may still show the date, even when vans, florists or individual bouquet lines are already at capacity.

TruBytes combines scheduling with day, product and time-slot capacity so the dates customers see can reflect what the business can actually fulfil.

FAQ

Florists delivery date questions.

Can I limit Valentine’s Day or Mother’s Day orders?

Yes. Daily order limits and product quantity limits can control the total workload and individual bouquet quantities for a date.

Can I charge more for a premium delivery day?

Yes. Daily surcharge rules can apply additional charges to selected methods and dates.

Can customers check whether local delivery is available to their postcode?

TruBytes local delivery rules can validate eligible postcode zones or radius-based areas as part of the local delivery workflow.

Can pickup and local delivery have different schedules?

Yes. Each fulfilment method can have its own available weekdays, lead times, blackout dates and time slots.

Where does the TruBytes storefront picker appear?

The current public app uses a Shopify cart-page app block before checkout. Cart-drawer, product-page and checkout date selection are not currently supported.
