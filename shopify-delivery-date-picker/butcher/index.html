FAQ

Butchers & fresh food delivery date questions.

Can TruBytes handle products that need extra preparation time?

Yes. Product lead-time and chill-chain rules can add preparation time to selected products or product groups.

Can local delivery have a minimum order and delivery charge?

Yes. Local delivery zones can include postcode or radius eligibility, minimum order values, delivery prices and allowed weekdays.

Can I limit how much fresh stock is sold for one delivery day?

Yes. Product quantity limits can cap individual products by day, alongside broader order and time-slot capacity limits.

Can I create production lists for a delivery date?

Yes. Selected orders can be turned into grouped production and picking lists so the team can see total quantities required for that date.

Where does the TruBytes storefront picker appear?

The current public app uses a Shopify cart-page app block before checkout. Cart-drawer, product-page and checkout date selection are not currently supported.
