Retailer Limits

Control purchase limits.

Set product and variant quantity limits, manage maximum cart weight and help prevent orders that exceed your rules.

Designed for straightforward purchasing rules

Retailer Limits lets merchants create quantity restrictions for specific products or variants and set a maximum cart weight. Configure rules in the Shopify admin and use checkout validation to enforce supported blocking rules.

What you can configure

  • Maximum quantities per product or variant, per order.
  • Maximum cart weight.
  • Custom checkout validation messages.
  • Enable or disable individual rules.

Getting started

Open the app in Shopify Admin, enable Retailer Limits, add your rules and save. Check that the Retailer Limits checkout rule is active under Shopify checkout settings. Test a cart that exceeds your chosen limit.
