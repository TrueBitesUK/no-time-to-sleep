Guide · Shopify Delivery Date

Let Shopify customers choose a delivery date without creating fulfilment problems.

The calendar is the easy part. The real work is deciding which dates should be available once cutoffs, preparation times, stock timing and daily capacity are taken into account.

Updated September 2026

A useful Shopify delivery-date setup should make a promise the business can actually keep. That means the available dates need to reflect the way orders are prepared, packed, collected or delivered.

Start with the fulfilment promise

Before choosing a date-picker implementation, write down the rules that determine when an order can leave the business. For example: which weekdays are available, how many lead days are needed, when the daily cutoff happens and whether local delivery follows a different schedule from shipping or pickup.

Use cutoffs to protect next-day delivery

If tomorrow's production run closes at noon, a customer ordering at 4pm should not be offered the same earliest date as somebody who ordered at 9am. A cutoff rule lets the store add extra lead time once the operational deadline has passed.

Let product preparation time override the store default

A store-wide two-day lead time breaks down when one product needs five days. Made-to-order cakes, fresh seafood, personalised gifts and seasonal products often need product-specific rules.

For mixed baskets, the safest available date is normally determined by the most restrictive product in the cart.

Use blackout and incoming dates for exceptions

Blackout dates handle closures, courier disruption and holidays. Incoming-stock dates handle the opposite problem: a product can be sold now but should not be delivered before the merchant expects it to arrive.

Limit work, not just stock

Delivery-date capacity can be based on total orders, total order value, individual product quantity or time-slot limits. These controls are useful when labour, vehicle space or production capacity is the real constraint.

Separate shipping, local delivery and pickup

Each fulfilment method can have a different operating calendar. A shop might ship Tuesday to Friday, offer Saturday pickup and run local delivery only on Wednesday and Friday. Treating those as separate schedules gives customers clearer choices and merchants more control.

Think about what happens after the customer chooses

For operational merchants, delivery scheduling should also make the order easier to fulfil. Viewing orders by delivery date and producing a grouped list of what needs preparing can be as valuable as the customer-facing calendar itself.
