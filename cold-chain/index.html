Temperature-controlled delivery for food ecommerce.

We're exploring whether independent food businesses can access refrigerated delivery more efficiently by connecting ecommerce demand with existing temperature-controlled carrier capacity.

I'm a food business I'm a refrigerated carrier
The problem

Food businesses are often simulating a cold chain.

Many smaller chilled and frozen ecommerce businesses rely on normal parcel networks combined with insulated packaging, liners and ice or gel packs.

That adds packaging cost, parcel weight and operational complexity. Delays and failed deliveries can become particularly expensive when the parcel contains perishable food.

We want to understand whether active refrigerated transport could offer a better overall solution.

The opportunity

Not another courier company.

The idea is to build the technology and network layer connecting merchants with refrigerated operators that already have vehicles, drivers and capacity.
